About Us

ConvertKaro is a free online toolkit for working with PDF and other everyday file formats. Our goal is simple: give people fast, reliable file tools — merge, split, compress, convert — without forcing a signup, a subscription, or a confusing interface.

What we offer

  • Merge, split, and organize PDF files
  • Compress PDFs while keeping quality intact
  • Convert between PDF, Word, Excel, and image formats
  • Sharpen and enhance photos

How it works

Many of our tools run entirely inside your browser — your files never leave your device for those. For conversions that need more processing power (like Word and Excel conversion), files are sent securely to our server, processed, and then deleted shortly after. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why we're free

ConvertKaro is supported by advertising, which lets us keep every tool free to use with no account required.
