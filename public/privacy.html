Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ConvertKaro ("we", "us") collects when you use convertkaro.com (the "Service"), and how that information is used.

1. Files you upload

Some tools on ConvertKaro (Merge, Split, JPG/PNG↔PDF, PDF→JPG, Enhance Image) run entirely in your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server.

Other tools (Compress, Word↔PDF, Excel↔PDF, PDF→Excel) require your file to be sent to our server for processing. These files are:

  • Used only to perform the requested conversion
  • Automatically deleted from our server immediately after processing, or within a short time if something fails
  • Never viewed by a human, sold, or shared with third parties

2. Cookies and advertising

ConvertKaro may display advertisements to keep the Service free. Our advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or via Google Ads Settings.

3. Analytics

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how the Service is used — for example, which tools are popular and how many people visit. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not require an account to use ConvertKaro, so we do not collect names, passwords, or payment information for using the core tools.

5. Children's privacy

ConvertKaro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email support.convertkaro@gmail.com.
